SPOT V3 BUILDER
1. Sign in and claim owner access once.
2. Open ADMIN.
3. Create or edit an auction.
4. Upload the real object photo.
5. Click ADD LOGO SPOT.
6. Drag the box directly over the object.
7. Drag the orange corner to resize it.
8. Set the starting bid and minimum increment.
9. Save as draft/upcoming/live.
10. When complete, mark ended/archived and optionally upload a proof photo.

The public auction uses the same x/y/width/height placement geometry saved by the visual builder.
