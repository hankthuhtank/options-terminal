Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what SPOT collects, why it is used, which service providers may process it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, SPOT may collect your email address, username, display name, company name, website, authentication identifiers, and account status.

Auction and bid information

SPOT records bids, bid amounts, bid timestamps, brand names, uploaded logos, auction participation, winning status, and related fulfillment information.

Support information

If you contact support, SPOT collects the information you provide in the request, including your name, email address, subject, and message.

Technical information

Service providers and hosting infrastructure may automatically process device, browser, network, security, session, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

SPOT uses information to create and secure accounts, display auctions, validate bids, identify winners, process or reconcile payments, fulfill physical placements, communicate service messages, respond to support requests, prevent abuse and fraud, comply with legal obligations, troubleshoot the service, and improve SPOT.

SPOT does not use account information to make unrelated automated eligibility decisions.

3. Service providers

SPOT relies on specialized providers to operate the service. These providers process information under their own contractual and privacy obligations.

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage, and realtime infrastructure.
  • Stripe may process payment information, payment methods, fraud signals, refunds, and transaction records when payments are enabled. Full card numbers are handled by Stripe rather than stored in SPOT's application database.
  • Hosting and delivery providers may process ordinary request and security logs required to serve the website.

SPOT may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer.

4. Information visible in auctions

SPOT is intentionally public in some areas. Brand names, winning logos, bid amounts, placement information, and completed-auction results may be shown publicly. A bidder's private email address and payment details are not intended to be displayed publicly.

If you submit a brand asset for an auction, understand that the asset may appear publicly on the auction, winner archive, and proof imagery if the bid wins.

5. Cookies and local storage

SPOT may use browser storage and authentication cookies or tokens that are necessary to keep users signed in, remember account state, secure requests, and operate core features. SPOT does not currently depend on third-party advertising trackers to operate its auction experience.

6. Data retention

Account and transaction records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, preserve auction history, meet tax or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Public winner archives may remain available as a record of completed auctions.

Support requests may be retained while they are relevant to customer service, security, payment, or legal needs.

7. Your privacy choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of eligible personal information through the Contact page. Some information may need to be retained where required for payment records, fraud prevention, legal compliance, completed auction history, or legitimate dispute resolution.

If privacy law in your location gives you additional rights, SPOT will honor those rights where applicable after reasonable identity verification.

SPOT does not sell personal information for money.

8. Security

SPOT uses access controls, row-level database policies, authenticated service operations, and third-party infrastructure designed to protect application data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should use unique passwords and promptly report suspicious activity.

9. Children

SPOT is not intended for children under 18 and does not knowingly permit minors to bid. If SPOT learns that personal information from a child was collected in error, reasonable steps will be taken to remove it.

10. Updates and contact

This policy may change as SPOT adds payment, auction, or account functionality. The date above will be updated when material changes are made. Privacy questions and requests can be submitted through the Contact & Support page.
