Payments & refunds.

SPOT's payment rules are designed around auctions: bidders know what is required before submitting a bid, winners know what they owe, and refunds follow clear triggers.

1. Payment processing

When payment functionality is enabled, SPOT may use Stripe to securely process payment methods, authorizations, charges, refunds, and related fraud checks. Full card numbers are handled by the payment processor rather than stored in SPOT's application database.

Payment availability can vary by country, card type, issuing bank, fraud checks, and processor rules.

2. What may be required before a bid

Depending on the auction, SPOT may require a valid payment method, a payment authorization, a refundable deposit, a partial payment, or another verification step before a bid is accepted.

The applicable amount and condition must be shown before the bidder confirms the bid. SPOT will not rely on a hidden deposit or fee that was not disclosed in the auction flow.

A temporary authorization is not necessarily a completed charge. The bidder's bank may display authorizations differently, and release timing is controlled partly by the bank and payment network.

3. Winning bidder payment

The winning bidder is responsible for the winning bid amount, less any deposit already credited toward the purchase, plus any applicable tax or clearly disclosed charge. If a remaining balance is due, SPOT may provide a deadline to complete payment.

Failure to pay by the deadline may result in cancellation of the award, loss of any non-refundable amount expressly disclosed before bidding, account restrictions, and reassignment or reopening of the placement.

4. If you are outbid

If the auction uses refundable bidder deposits, an outbid bidder's eligible deposit will be released or refunded according to the terms shown for that auction. Processor and bank posting times vary, so a released authorization or issued refund may not appear instantly.

If the auction uses a saved payment method without collecting a deposit, being outbid does not by itself create a charge.

5. Refund policy

Winning auction payments are generally non-refundable once the placement has been approved and fulfillment has begun, because the purchase is for a customized physical advertising placement. This does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

SPOT may issue a full or partial refund where appropriate, including when SPOT cancels an auction, cannot provide the awarded placement, materially misrepresented the placement, receives a duplicate payment, or determines that a refund is otherwise required by law.

A refund may be denied where a bidder simply changes their mind after winning, fails to provide compliant artwork, or violates the auction rules, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

6. Declined, failed, or reversed payments

If a payment method is declined or a charge cannot be completed, the bidder may be asked to provide another valid payment method within a reasonable deadline. SPOT may withhold fulfillment until payment is successfully completed.

If a completed payment is later reversed or disputed without valid basis, SPOT may pause fulfillment, remove the placement, suspend the account, or pursue amounts legitimately owed.

7. Payment questions and disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact SPOT through the support page before initiating a payment dispute so the transaction can be reviewed. Nothing in this policy prevents a cardholder from exercising rights provided by their bank, card network, or applicable law.

Before launch: each live auction will display its exact payment model—such as card-on-file, deposit, or winner-only charge—directly in the bidding flow.
