What SPOT protects

01

Payment data

When Stripe is enabled, full card details are handled by the payment processor rather than stored in the SPOT application database.

02

Account data

Authentication and private application records are protected with database access controls and authenticated operations.

03

Auction integrity

Bid validation is performed server-side so the browser cannot simply declare itself the high bidder.

Before opening a payment dispute

If a transaction looks wrong, submit a support request first. SPOT can review the auction record, bid history, payment state, and fulfillment status. This does not take away any rights you have through your bank, card network, or applicable law.
