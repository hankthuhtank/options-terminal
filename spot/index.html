HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. That’s it.

Pick the physical placement you want, add a secure payment method, upload your brand, and bid. Winning bids are binding and charged when the auction closes.

01

Choose a spot

Click any outlined placement directly on the current object.

02

03

Winning bidders are charged at close and approved logos are applied to the real object.
