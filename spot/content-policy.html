Brand Content Policy.

SPOT puts winning brand assets onto real objects. That makes content approval part of fulfillment, not an afterthought.

1. You must have rights to the brand

Only submit a logo, trademark, business name, image, slogan, URL, or other asset that you own or are authorized to use. By submitting content, you represent that the content does not infringe another party's copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, or other rights.

2. Generally acceptable content

Ordinary lawful business branding, creator branding, product branding, event branding, organizational marks, URLs, and promotional artwork are generally eligible when they accurately represent the bidder and can be reproduced within the placement.

3. Content SPOT may reject

SPOT may reject or require replacement of content that includes or promotes:

  • illegal products, services, conduct, or instructions;
  • fraud, impersonation, deceptive claims, phishing, malware, or scams;
  • unlicensed third-party trademarks or copyrighted artwork;
  • credible threats, targeted harassment, or incitement to violence;
  • hateful or dehumanizing content targeting protected characteristics;
  • explicit sexual imagery or pornographic material;
  • graphic gore or imagery unsuitable for public physical display;
  • content designed to create confusion about SPOT's endorsement, ownership, or affiliation;
  • content that would make physical placement unlawful in the location where the object is used.

SPOT may also decline content that creates a material safety, reputational, legal, or fulfillment problem even if it is not specifically listed above.

4. Artwork quality and format

Bidders should provide clean, high-resolution artwork with sufficient contrast for the physical object. Vector artwork or transparent-background PNG files may be requested when needed for production. Minor sizing, cropping, padding, or color-profile adjustments may be made to fit the awarded zone without materially changing the brand.

5. Review and replacement

A high bid does not force SPOT to reproduce prohibited content. If a winning asset cannot be approved, SPOT may ask the bidder to provide a compliant replacement within a reasonable period. If the bidder cannot or will not provide acceptable artwork, the placement may be cancelled under the Payments & Refund Policy and Auction Rules.

6. Limited license to fulfill and document the placement

By submitting brand content, you grant SPOT a limited, non-exclusive, worldwide license to host, display, resize, reproduce, print, apply, photograph, and show that content as reasonably necessary to operate the auction, fulfill a winning placement, display auction results, and document the object “in the wild.”

You retain ownership of your brand content. SPOT does not acquire the underlying trademark or copyright merely because the asset was submitted or won a placement.

Best practice: upload the same logo your company publicly uses, in the highest-quality file you have.
