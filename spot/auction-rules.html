THE GAME / RULES

Pay more. Take it.

TAKEOVER is deliberately simple: twelve pieces of one webpage, controlled by whoever is willing to pay the most for each piece right now.

1. Take a spot

An empty spot starts at the price displayed on the page. An occupied spot can be taken at the displayed minimum takeover price. The default takeover increase is $1, although the operator may change the global increment prospectively.

You may voluntarily enter a higher level than the minimum. The amount shown at Checkout is the amount you are agreeing to pay.

2. Keep it until somebody pays more

There is no daily reset and no guaranteed minimum run time. Once a valid payment assigns the spot to you, the spot remains yours until another valid takeover replaces it, the advertisement is removed for a policy reason, or the service is materially changed or discontinued.

3. Grow into connected territory

You can purchase multiple touching spots in one transaction. Connected spots controlled by the same advertiser may visually merge into a larger advertisement. Each numbered spot still keeps its own price and may later be taken individually.

4. Defend your spot

If you currently own a spot, you may raise its level before somebody takes it. You pay only the difference between the spot's current level and the new level you choose. A defense does not create a guaranteed time period; it simply raises the amount a challenger must beat.

5. Take the whole page

The TAKE THE PAGE control selects all twelve spots at their then-current takeover requirements. Spots you already own are included without charging you for them again. If the purchase succeeds, your advertising can occupy the full page.

A full-page takeover is not permanent. Another advertiser can break it by successfully taking any individual spot at a higher level.

6. When two people try at once

Prices are checked by the server before Checkout begins and checked again after Stripe reports payment. The first qualifying completed transaction to lock the affected spot or spots wins.

If you pay based on an outdated board and the quoted territory is no longer available at your paid levels, TAKEOVER rejects that stale purchase and attempts an automatic refund. This prevents two advertisers from being assigned the same spot at the same time.

7. What is public

The page publicly shows the advertising content and current price of each spot. Private account identifiers, payment information, and losing purchase attempts are not intended to be public.

8. Moderation still applies

Paying the highest amount does not override the Advertising Content Policy. TAKEOVER may remove unlawful, malicious, infringing, deceptive, impersonating, or otherwise prohibited advertisements.
