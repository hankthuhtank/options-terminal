Our Philosophy

A Safe Space for Everyone

We are committed to creating a safe, supportive space where every patient feels heard, valued, and respected. Your journey is unique, and we honor that by working together to find a treatment plan that fits your individual needs.

Founder & Owner

Kaitlin Baker

MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC

Kaitlin Baker is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with a Master's of Science in Nursing, specializing in psychiatric medication management. With 15 years in the medical field and the last 5 dedicated to psychiatry, she founded Northeast Texas Behavioral Health with a vision to bring accessible, compassionate mental health care to the Paris community and surrounding areas.

Kaitlin diagnoses and treats mental health conditions with both medication and therapy, creating personalized treatment plans that address the whole person. She is passionate about helping patients feel heard, supported, and comfortable while working together to develop strategies that fit their unique lives. She treats adults 18 and older.

How We Help

Conditions We Treat

We provide evaluation and medication management for adults experiencing a wide range of mental health concerns. You don't have to face these challenges alone.

Anxiety

Persistent worry, panic attacks, social anxiety, and generalized anxiety disorders that interfere with daily life.

Depression

Feelings of hopelessness, loss of interest, fatigue, and mood changes that affect your quality of life.

ADHD

Difficulty focusing, impulsivity, and hyperactivity in adults that may have gone undiagnosed for years.

Sleep Difficulties

Insomnia, disrupted sleep patterns, and sleep-related issues that impact mental and physical health.

Mood Disorders

Bipolar disorder, persistent mood instability, and other conditions affecting emotional regulation.

Other Concerns

Schizophrenia, PTSD, OCD, and additional mental health conditions requiring professional evaluation and care.

Not sure if we can help? Reach out anyway. If we aren't the right fit, we'll help guide you toward the care you need.
