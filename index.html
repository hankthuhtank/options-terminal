03 / TRADING EDUCATION

The Trading Desk.

No signals, no hype, no "get rich by Friday." Just the structure I had to learn the slow, expensive way — taught one-on-one, so the market makes sense before your money's on the line.

I blew it before I built it.

I didn't come to this with a plan. I came in chasing green candles and social-media hype, and the market did what it does to everyone who starts that way — it took its tuition. Stretches of losses, revenge trades that dug the hole deeper, the whole cycle.

What turned it around wasn't a magic indicator. It was structure — understanding risk, sizing, the psychology, and most of all learning when not to trade. Consistency came after the lessons I wish someone had just walked me through.

That's the whole point of this program: a shortcut through the expensive part.
