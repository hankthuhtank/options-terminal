I'm a first-generation Lebanese-American based in Paris, Texas. Most of my background is in customer-facing work and operations, so I tend to look at a project from both sides — what makes sense for the business and what actually feels good for the person using it.

Safi Solutions started with websites, but I realized pretty quickly that I enjoy the problem-solving part more than any one service. That has grown into custom websites, SaaS concepts, educational platforms, business tools, and whatever else seems worth building.

A lot of my work starts the same way: I find something confusing, clunky, or overlooked and start picking it apart until I can make it simpler, more visual, or more useful. Some of those ideas become my own projects; others are things I build for clients.

I'm always learning new industries and new tools, and that's honestly part of what I like most about it. The common thread is still simple: find the friction and build something better around it.
