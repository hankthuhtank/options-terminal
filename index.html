06 / THE PERSON BEHIND IT

Still the same Safi. A much bigger toolbox.

I'm Helal Safi, a first-generation Lebanese-American out of Paris, Texas. My background is a mix of customer-facing work, management, operations, computer science, economics and a tendency to keep asking, “Why does this have to work this way?”

The original Safi Solutions was mostly websites, marketing and saving businesses money. Building those projects kept pulling me toward the part I enjoyed most: finding the actual friction and designing the thing that removes it.

Today that might be a distinctive website. It might be an interactive education platform. It might be a SaaS prototype that eventually replaces five tools.

The category matters less than the outcome: make something useful, clear and worth remembering.
