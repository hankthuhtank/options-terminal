THE PERSON BEHIND THE LAB

I learned by building things I wished existed.

Safi Solutions started much more broadly around websites, marketing and helping businesses save money. The work kept pulling in a different direction: products, interfaces, software concepts and unusual ways of making complicated things easier.

That is the direction now. I use modern development tools and AI aggressively behind the scenes, but the goal is not to make “AI-looking” work. The goal is to move faster while still making deliberate decisions about the problem, structure, visuals and experience.
