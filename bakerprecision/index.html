Why a Home Inspection Matters

A professional inspection is your best protection against unexpected costs and safety hazards. Here's what every homeowner should know before signing on the dotted line.

01

The Foundation Truth

In Texas and Oklahoma, clay soils expand and contract dramatically with moisture changes. This shifting ground causes foundation movement that can lead to cracks, uneven floors, and structural issues. A thorough inspection identifies early warning signs before they become $10,000+ repairs.

02

Roof Reality Check

Hail, high winds, and intense sun exposure are harsh realities in this region. Many roofs show surface-level damage that's invisible from the ground. We examine shingles, flashing, ventilation, and decking to estimate remaining lifespan and catch leaks before they destroy ceilings and insulation.

03

Electrical Safety

Outdated wiring, overloaded panels, and DIY electrical work are common in older homes. These pose serious fire hazards. Our inspection covers the main panel, subpanels, outlets, grounding systems, and GFCI protection to ensure your family's safety.

04

Plumbing & Drainage

Hidden leaks can waste thousands of gallons and breed mold behind walls. We check water pressure, drainage slopes, water heater condition, and pipe materials. In our region, slab leaks are especially common and costly if missed.

05

HVAC Efficiency

Texas summers and Oklahoma winters push heating and cooling systems to their limits. An inefficient or failing HVAC unit can cost hundreds monthly and fail when you need it most. We evaluate system age, capacity, ductwork, and performance.

06

Your Negotiation Power

An inspection report isn't just about walking away—it's leverage. Documented issues can lead to seller repairs, price reductions, or closing credits. Going in blind means accepting all the risk. Going in informed means protecting your investment.

What Gets Inspected

Every major system and structural component is evaluated with precision. Here's a breakdown of what your inspection covers.

Structural Integrity

Foundation, framing, floors, walls, and ceilings. We look for cracks, settlement, moisture intrusion, and signs of past or ongoing structural stress.

Includes crawl spaces, basements, and attic framing

Roof & Exterior

Roofing materials, gutters, downspouts, siding, windows, doors, and grading. We assess weather damage, proper drainage, and exterior protective systems.

Hail damage assessment included where visible

Electrical Systems

Service panels, breakers, wiring types, outlets, switches, and fixtures. We identify safety hazards like aluminum wiring, double-tapped breakers, and missing GFCI protection.

Safety-focused evaluation per NEC standards

Plumbing & Water

Supply lines, drains, vents, water heaters, fixtures, and water pressure. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper venting, and adequate hot water capacity.

Includes functional flow and drainage testing

HVAC Systems

Heating and cooling equipment, ductwork, filters, thermostats, and ventilation. We test operation, temperature differential, and airflow to gauge system health.

Seasonal performance evaluation

Interior Details

Walls, ceilings, floors, stairs, railings, cabinets, countertops, and built-in appliances. We note material conditions, safety issues, and signs of concealed problems.

Includes window operation and door seal checks
